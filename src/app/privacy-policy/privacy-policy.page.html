Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us (such as name, email, and payment details during booking or signup), as well as verification documents from hosts. We also collect usage data automatically as you navigate the app.

2. How We Use Information

We use your data to process bookings, facilitate communication between guests and hosts, improve our services, enforce our Terms of Service, and provide customer support.

3. Data Sharing

We share necessary information (like name and contact details) between guests and hosts upon a confirmed booking. We may share data with payment processors and required legal authorities, but we never sell your personal data to third-party advertisers.

4. Data Security

We implement robust technical and organizational measures (including Supabase row-level security and encryption) to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

5. User Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal data. You can manage this in your Profile Settings or by submitting a request to our support team.

6. Cookies and Tracking

We use local storage and cookies to maintain your active session, remember your preferences, and analyze app performance.

7. Contact Information

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@tripmate.ph or via our Contact Us page.
