Built in Quezon City, for every island.

TripMate connects travelers with vetted hotels and house rentals across the Philippines, booked direct from the people who live there.

Planning a trip shouldn't run on screenshots and hope.

Booking a stay in the Philippines has too often meant messaging a page, waiting for a reply, and sending a GCash payment to someone you've never met. We built TripMate to make that simpler, and safer.

Every host submits a valid ID and proof of property before their first listing goes live. Every review comes from a guest who completed a paid booking. And you pay the way you already do: GCash, Maya, or bank transfer.

We're based in Quezon City and we build for the whole archipelago. From Batanes to Siargao, the best stays are run by people who live there. TripMate gives them a place to be found, and gives you a reason to trust what you see.

What we stand for.

Four commitments that shape how TripMate is built and who we build it with.

Local first
Our listings come from hosts who live where you're headed, so you get their knowledge as well as their spare room.
Trust before the booking
IDs and proof of property are checked before a listing goes live, not after a complaint comes in.
Honest reviews
Only guests with a completed, paid booking can leave a review. That's the whole rule, and it keeps the five stars real.
Built for how Filipinos pay
GCash, Maya, and bank transfer are first-class options, not an afterthought behind a credit card form.
